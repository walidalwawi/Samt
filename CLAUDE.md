# Walid Al Wawi · CLAUDE.md

## What this is
Static site for walidalwawi.com on Cloudflare Workers (static assets, free plan). Pushing to `main` deploys.
Source of truth is `content/site.json`; the back office at /admin renders and publishes it.

## Back office (site/admin.html + site/admin/grid.*)
Walid's Site Grid editor — ONE back office for walidalwawi.com and samt.co. Identical files in both repos (site/admin.html differs only in BD_HOST); change one, copy to the other. BD_SITES in admin.html lists the sites; the switcher stores the choice in localStorage bd-site-current. One GitHub key (bd-gh-key) covers both repos.
- Blocks: text, image, gallery, library, link, notes, list, video, html, rule, quote, fold-out list (toggle), product; site blocks menu (list or button + overlay, with brand text beside it), page title, page arrows (arrows or "Next, title" words; pages or blog scope); blog blocks posts (cards grid/columns/list, featured, card look) and post info (back link, words ~ minutes).
- Blogs: a page with `blog: true`; posts are pages with `parent: <blog id>`, plus `date`, `excerpt`, `featured`. Posts are skipped by the menu and by site-scope page arrows. Cards use the post's h1 || title, first image, and word count.
- Prefer a real block over an html block; html blocks are for genuine one-offs.
- Inspect: picks any element in the canvas; design bar writes rules into site.css or page.css via cssSplit/ruleSet; on Phone into the single @media (max-width: 760px) block, which Publish merges into bd.css's one mobile block.
- Code drawer is CodeMirror 5 (cdnjs). Files tab edits any non-generated repo file and uploads to site/files/ or site/images/; both ride in the publish commit.
- Loads content/site.json from GitHub with Walid's fine-grained token (kept in his browser only).
- Draft autosaves to localStorage (bd-draft-<repo>) until Publish.
- Publish = one commit: content/site.json, content/manifest.json, every page .html, site/404.html, site/assets/bd.css (stamped "PUBLISHED <id>"), site/assets/bd.js, new images (uploaded earlier as git blobs). Pages in the old manifest but not the new one are deleted. Then polls /assets/bd.css for the stamp.
- Image props: assetId "live" (in site/images), "sha:<blob>" (uploaded, not yet published), "remote" (still hot-linked from an old URL; Publish tries to copy it in).

## Layers (site.layers.on + site.sheets) — the paper-sheet site (used by samt.co)
Any page can lie on a translucent sheet; site.sheets is the stack, top → bottom (list order = z order). Pages not on a sheet lie underneath (content layer), one shown at a time.
- A sheet (SHEET_DEFAULTS): pages (ordered; several = it slides between them, axis y/x), rest (open | still | peek | hidden; still = never moves, lies where it is like a card in a stack), restPage (with rest=peek: the page drawn on the edge, always there — sheetStrip(k); the other pages are the panes, sheetPanes(k)), edge/edgeM (where it parks, desktop/phone), peek/peekM px (how much of the rest page shows), sliver/sliverM px (what an open sheet leaves showing when a page underneath opens; from its own edge plus the strips of peeking sheets above on that edge, so it is visible beside them; out=hide ignores it), covers (all | some + coverPages), color/alpha/blur/shadow (none | line | hard; was `line`), nudge (px a peeking sheet slides out on hover, desktop with hover, not over a link), dur. Older fields strip / paneW / pad / padM migrate on load (strip → pages + restPage; paneW/pad → the pane pages' box maxW/pl/pr).
- Menu+About and Journal+Library are the same thing: a sheet with two pages. The menu is a page ("Menu") holding a Sheet menu block (sheetmenu: menu, marker, align, style, pad/padM, gap/gapM, mark); it is the Menu sheet's rest page.
- Where it's edited: Pages tab → "+ Sheet" (a sheet with one fresh page), sheets show as groups with their pages indented ("edge" tag = rest page). Tapping a sheet's title in Pages selects the sheet (S.sheetSel): the right panel becomes the sheet panel (sheetPanelHTML → sheetEditHTML, the ONLY place sheet settings live: pages + spread axis, Paper = fill (None / Solid / Gradient with a clear end) + see-through + blur + pattern (lined / squared / dotted / ruled; sheetPattern, sheetPaper) + edge, Margin & padding (k.box; margin px or %, padding px, phone M variants; sheetEx / sheetPadIns), movement (parks at, at rest, leave = peeks or hides (k.out), Leaves with = per-sheet transition k.lv → class is-lv-<t>, Appears slide / fade / cut → is-ap-*, speed), lies over). Tapping a page returns the right panel to that page; a page lying on a sheet has a Sheet settings button (to put a page on a sheet, drag it from Layers onto the sheet's drawing). The sheet panel also holds Stack position (Higher / Lower) and Dissolve; the Site tab only has the on/off switch and a count (no sheet settings anywhere else). Motion tab = how every sheet moves by default; a sheet overrides speed and leave in its own panel.
- A page lying on a sheet has an inset (page.box m*), not a margin: see Page inset below. Padding/card stay on the panel (pageBoxCSS with noMargin). A page with a max width on a sheet sits at the left; underneath it centres. For pages underneath, margins inset the panel and sheetBg fills behind.
- Blur lives only on sheets (backdrop-filter, sheetBlur = sqrt(own blur² + "comes into focus" blur²)); the content layer is never filtered, so what a smaller sheet doesn't cover stays sharp.
- PAPER (every sheet; strips are legacy): the sheet is ONE sheet of paper. Its margins (k.box m*) belong to the paper, not the frame: the frame (.bd-ly-k.is-paper) is window-sized and never moves with scrolling. Each pane holds `.bd-ly-paper` (background, blur, shadow, margins, min-height) around `.bd-panel`. Along-axis start margin sits on the first pane, end margin on the last pane (bottom = a `.bd-ly-pane::after` spacer via --pm-b), cross-axis margins on every pane (sheetPaperSides / sheetPaperMargins / sheetEx). Vertical margins scroll with the content, so scrolling moves paper and borders together, and dragging the bottom edge in the layout drawing moves the bottom of the whole sheet (seen only on its last page). Strip sheets (rest=peek, e.g. Menu) keep the fixed-frame behaviour: margins, blur and shadow on the frame, with the rise-first scroll (--k-up/--k-mt in bdLayers). Blur on paper sheets sits on .bd-ly-paper (backdrop-filter inside a scroller: check Safari).
- Sheets only ever slide in a straight line: a sheet keeps the margins of the page it shows while it tucks/hides (onPage; strips only, paper sheets keep their margins inside the paper), and its box changes instantly (is-snapbox) whenever its state changes, so top/left/right/bottom never animate during a slide.
- LEGACY (strips are no longer made by the UI; layUnparkIn converts old ones once, the engine still renders rest=peek if a site.json has one): Sheet behaviour is the drawn geometry (layWrite): the ★ page is the page the window sits on at rest (k.main; for a strip, k.restPage). If the ★ page is first/last and the sheet's margin on that side cuts into it (the other pages lie beyond the window), the ★ page is either a strip (rest=peek, edge = the window side the sheet occupies, peek = the visible px, that side's box margin stays blank) or a page (rest still/open with the cut stored as a box margin) — the "The ★ page is" choice under the drawing (lay-strip, k.layPg remembers an explicit "page"; a new cut below 50% defaults to strip). ▽ (data-lh=away) = where the sheet goes when a page underneath opens: dragged onto itself = rest still; dragged out = rest open + edge + out peek/hide + sliver (visible px minus the peeking strips on that edge). Hidden at rest is a seg in Exact numbers (lay-hide). Phone drags write only *M numbers; what a sheet is (strip/page/still/open) is decided on the desktop. sheetMove / sheetMoveState remain as a programmatic API (tests) but have no UI.
- Page inset replaces page margin on sheets: page.box m* on a page lying ON a sheet is an inset (insetCSS: margin on the panel inside the pane, the sheet's paper shows around; pane stays full). It no longer moves the sheet's edges (sheetMarginCSS removed). Underneath pages keep real margins. The rise-first scroll (--k-mt / --k-up) now applies to strip sheets only; paper sheets scroll their margins natively. Canvas: .bd-site.is-layered::before paints the paper inside the sheet's own margins; the panel carries the inset.
- Sheet panel (settings pane, 420px for a sheet; sheetEditHTML) order: Layout (name · layout drawing · legend · spread axis · + page chooser) · Style (Background colour / effect / decoration) · Animation (On appear = k.arrive, On leave = k.lv, On hover = k.hover none | lift → is-hv-lift; desktop hover only; an old nudge value is cleared by sheetFix) · Exact numbers (folded: margins, padding, slides-away edge / left-showing px for open sheets, At rest As drawn | Hidden) · Lies over · Stack. There is no Behaviour section any more. A page on a sheet inherits the sheet's style; it can set its own card colour / see-through / blur, `page.pattern` (inherit | none | pattern, pagePaperK) and `page.hover` (lift). Sheets stack in panel order (z-index), and anything smaller than the window or see-through shows what lies under it.
- Paper is painted on the panes (and the edge strip), not on the sheet box: a gradient uses `local` attachment and is sized N× the pane so it runs over the whole scroll area and continues across the sheet's pages (sheetPaper(k, pct, j, N); axis y/x). Pattern layers sit on top of it.
- Phone: a page's own padding overrides the desktop peek inset (pageBoxCSS `passed` flag), so the Journal never keeps the menu's 300px on a phone.
- Layout drawing (sheetGeo → layoutDrawing; layApply → layWrite; the ONLY place anything is dragged): the panel order is Pages spread (Vertical/Horizontal) → the drawing → + page → hint. No "At rest / A page underneath opens" thumbnails and no caption sentence (layResult / layCaption are gone). The canvas is ALWAYS two windows wide and two high — four window-sized halves (1280×800 / 390×780), the window cell highlighted. Tapping a half (data-act=lay-cell, data-c/data-r; k.vc/k.vr remember the cell) puts the window there; the sheet always covers two neighbouring halves (a column for axis y, a row for axis x — page 1 on the first half, page 2 on the second) and the tapped half is the ★ page (k.main, via layWrite star). The JOIN between the two pages always lies on a window edge, never inside the window. Dragging a page card across the join swaps the two pages (data-swap; a tap still puts the window there). The handles are the sheet's four outer edges (`[data-lh=sheet]`: along-axis sides = mStart/mEnd = the start margin of the first page / end margin of the last page, across = box margins on every page; snap 5%) — dragging the bottom of a vertical sheet moves the end of the sheet, seen only at the end of the last page's scroll. No seam, no ▼, no ▽, no page handles, no strip/peek written by the UI (layWrite never creates rest=peek). layUnparkIn (every load) turns an old parked strip (rest=peek) into plain paper: rest=still, main=restPage, the visible slice kept as a margin at the outer end of the ★ page (e.g. peek 300 → right margin 76.56%; phone from edgeM/peekM into the M keys). Exact numbers hold 'Slides away to' (edge), 'Left showing when it slides away', margins/padding. Tapping a page = Layers list.
- Drawing look (like the Open/Closed clip): the drawing sits in a soft .bd-lay-box; pages are white rounded cards with skeleton lines, labelled under each card (x axis) or inside (y axis); outside the window they are faded (dimPass), inside it bright (brightPass, clipPath bd-lay-win-<n>); the window is a rounded orange (#d9573b) frame. Handles and data attributes are unchanged.
- Two pages per sheet (sheetFull; blog posts never count): + page, New page, sheet-page-add and dragging in Layers refuse a third. sheetFix(p) (load + changed()) drops missing page ids and keeps a strip where the export puts it (left/top edge = last, right/bottom = first), so the drawing reads like the real site (About, then Menu).
- Scrollbars (scrollCSS, /* SCROLLBARS */): thin black line everywhere (scrollbar-width/color + ::-webkit-scrollbar). With layers on the native bars of panes and the content layer are hidden and one draggable black line (#bd-ly-bar, runtime bar()) sits on the window's far right edge for whichever page is scrolling.
- Fills: a sheet's colour, a page's card colour and "behind the page" can be a solid or a two-colour linear gradient (string "linear-gradient(<n>deg, #a, #b)"; fillIn UI = Solid | Gradient via the `fill.<part>|<path>` pseudo-paths in applyPath; parseFill/fillCSS apply the sheet's see-through to both stops; the value "none" paints nothing and a gradient stop may be "transparent" (fill.ends pseudo-part); bgVal() maps none → transparent).
- A sheet that parks on an edge (rest=peek) is inset by the peeks of the peeking sheets above it (sheetBoxIns); every other sheet starts at the window's edge and lies UNDER them (translucent), and only its pages' content is padded by those peeks (pageBoxCSS ins + noMargin). Page margins are measured from the window edge. Pages underneath are padded by all peeking sheets.
- bdSheetPlan(K, focus, prev) — exported as-is, used by Publish (initial classes) and the runtime: the page's own sheet opens on it; sheets above go back to rest (open-at-rest ones tuck); sheets below keep what they were doing; a page underneath tucks every open-at-rest sheet; a sheet that doesn't cover the page hides. Runtime bdLayers(): is-st-open/peek/tuck/hidden on .bd-ly-k, page on a sheet = --i on .bd-ly-track (snaps instantly when the sheet isn't open, so it only slides back), pushState, Escape/back, About-style toggle.
- Opening transitions (LAYER_TRANS, site.layers.trans; page.trans overrides — Space & card → Opens with, pages underneath only): slide, fold, unfold, squash, drop, push, rise, cut — sheetStateCSS writes every state per edge and per transition (desktop prefix is-e-, phone is-em- inside the one mobile block). page.transDur → --ly-pdur.
- Preview pane: with a sheet selected (S.sheetSel, S.spread !== false, not Preview) the canvas shows the SPREAD — spreadDoc(n, phone) in an iframe.bd-spread: the whole sheet laid flat at real px (exportCSS + layerBody per page in .bd-sp-slot > .bd-ly-pane[data-page], the strip page restyled as a pane), other sheets as dashed ghosts, the away ghost, the window frame (.bd-sp-win), a tap overlay per page (.bd-sp-tap → postMessage bdSpreadPage → goPage). fitSheet zooms down to 12% for it. The ruler offers Spread | Window (spread-view / window-view = S.preview). Canvas: a page on a sheet is drawn as the sheet shrunk to the page's margins on a grey desk (layerEditCSS; % = share of the drawn 1280×800 / 390×780 window via BOX_VIEW), with the peeking sheets above drawn at their edges (layerEditChrome renders their rest pages); the edge page is drawn at least 120 px / 40 px (phone) even when peek is 0. Before: the page on its sheet's paper, with the peeking sheets above drawn at their edges (layerEditChrome renders their rest pages); a rest page is edited at strip size (.bd-site.is-strip). Preview renders the real export in an iframe (kept in place; see Panels).
- Site → On every page: Page title at the top (site.header; a page switches itself off with page.header=false) and Click images to zoom (site.zoom; an image opts out with props.zoom=false). followV1 (once) clears the old blanket per-page / per-image overrides so the site switches work. With layers on and one menu, Site shows just that menu's links (no picker).
- Classic set: migrateSheets runs once when layers are on and site.sheets is missing. The old three-sheet output is pixel-identical (tests/states.py).

## Pinned page, Home, build colour
- Pinned page (sheet.pin = page id, set in the sheet panel "Pinned page"): the page never leaves the window. The sheet still slides between its two pages, but sheetPinCSS clamps the track transform (`clamp(-1, calc(var(--i) - pi), 1) * L`, L = window − the pinned page's outer margin, kept ≥ 48px short of the window) so the pinned page stops against the window edge; the other pane gets padding of L so its scroll reaches its end. A pinned sheet is forced rest=still, sorted to the top of site.sheets (sheetPinOrder), never hidden/tucked (bdSheetPlan: `pin > -1`), returns to its ★ page when a page underneath opens, lets clicks through around its paper (pointer-events), and pads the pages underneath by L on its edge unless pinPad="off" (sheetInsets, nominal px). Once (site.pinV1): the page holding the sheet menu is pinned.
- Whole-sheet scrolling: with a pinned page the sheet scrolls as ONE. The open (other) pane scrolls; runtime bdLayers copies its scrollTop/scrollLeft to --k-sy/--k-sx on the pinned pane, which moves with it (transform: translate3d(-sx·f, -sy·f); f = 1 while the other page is open). Only the slide keeps the pinned page in the window. Pinned FIRST: the pinned pane is shifted by L with the `translate` property (transitioned) and the other pane has padding-start L (its paper starts below/after the pinned page; scrolling moves both); pinned LAST: the track transform is clamped and the other pane has padding-start L. Wheel over the pinned page is forwarded to the open pane.
- Independent page (page.indep; "This page is" Part of the sheet | Independent in the page's Space & card): part of the sheet = the sheet's paper and scrolls with the sheet; independent = own card colour / behind / corners / see-through / blur / shadow / decoration / hover, plus (only on a pinned sheet) page.scroll "sheet" (default) | "own". "own" marks the pane `data-own` (runtime skips the scroll proxy; with the pinned page first it is clipped under the pinned page via clip-path). Switching back to Part of the sheet clears the own look (action page-indep). Once (site.indepV1) pages on sheets that already had an own look become independent.
- A page on a sheet shows "Where it lies · <sheet>" in its own panel: the same drawing (layoutDrawing(n, pg, "page")) with the four handles on THAT page's margins (kind page → layApply writes page.box m*) instead of the sheet's edges; the whole sheet stays drawn for reference.
- Pinned page STAYS PUT (sheet.pinMode "" slides | "stay"; k.pinModeM "" same as desktop | slide | stay; sheetPinStay): the pinned pane cancels the track slide with `translate: calc((var(--i) - pi) * 100%)` and lies above (z-index 2); the other pane keeps clear of the band with padding on the side opposite the pinned page's big (≥50%) margin (computed in sheetPinCSS from the pinned page's own margins); pages underneath are not padded (sheetPinPx null). Used for the phone Menu band: Menu stays at the bottom, About fills the screen above it and scrolls on its own.
- An independent page on a pinned sheet OWNS ITS PAPER: its own page.box margins are the paper's margins (sheetPaperMargins) and replace the sheet's margins for that pane; no inset is written (layerPageCSS skips insetCSS).
